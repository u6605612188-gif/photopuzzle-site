Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PhotoPuzzle handles information when you use our mobile game. We designed PhotoPuzzle to work with minimal data and to keep your gameplay simple and private.

Information we collect

  • Account information (optional): If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and basic profile to save progress and enable VIP. You can play without signing in.
  • Advertising & device data: Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, an advertising ID and app-usage signals to show and measure ads.
  • Analytics & diagnostics: We use Google Firebase to keep the app stable (crash and basic usage data).
  • Purchases: VIP is processed by Google Play Billing. We receive a purchase confirmation to activate VIP — we never see your payment details.
Your photos stay with you. Photos you pick to build a puzzle are used on your device only. We do not upload, store or share your personal photos.

How we use information

  • To provide and improve the game (levels, daily challenges, global ranking).
  • To save your progress and activate VIP when you sign in.
  • To display and measure advertising.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

Third-party services

PhotoPuzzle uses trusted Google services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing & Sign-In — policies.google.com/privacy

Children

PhotoPuzzle is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data retention & your rights

If you signed in, your progress and VIP status are linked to your account. You can request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting us. Advertising and analytics data are retained by the respective providers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
